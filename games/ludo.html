How to play

RollTap the die, or press Space on your turn
MoveTap a glowing token, or press 1–4
Leave the yardNeeds a roll of 6
CaptureLand on a rival token (not on a star square) to send it home
Safe squaresStarred squares can't be captured on
Extra turnRolling a 6, capturing, or finishing a token
Three sixesThree in a row forfeits the turn
WinFirst to bring all four tokens home
